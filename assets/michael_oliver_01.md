========= Michael Oliver Gold Silver Are Entering Their Next Phase.srt =========

### Overview

In this interview, Michael Oliver, founder of Momentum Structural Analysis, outlines a looming systemic financial crisis fueled by unprecedented government, corporate, and private debt. Oliver uses his signature momentum-based technical analysis to predict a major asset rotation: money will flee overvalued stock markets and eroding bond markets to seek refuge in precious metals. He forecasts an imminent, explosive, and vertical rise in gold, silver, and particularly gold miners, which are currently trading at historic undervalutions.

---

### Key Takeaways

* **Explosive Metals Rally Ahead:** Gold and silver are entering a new, highly vertical phase of growth, moving past the manipulative-looking sell-offs seen earlier this year.
* **Miners Ready to Supercharge:** Gold and silver miners (GDX/XAU) are historically cheap relative to physical gold; a breakout in the miner-to-gold ratio will cause miners to vastly outperform bullion. 
* **Fragile Stock Market Bubble:** The S&P 500 and Nasdaq are dangerously top-heavy. Oliver warns that a drop of roughly 10% will shatter major momentum support structures and accelerate a panic.
* **Bond Market Out of Control:** Long-term US Treasury prices are continually eroding, forcing the Fed to relentlessly print money to prop up the debt market, further degrading the purchasing power of fiat currency.
* **A Multi-Front Debt Crisis:** Unlike the isolated mortgage crisis of 2008, the current environment faces simultaneous defaults and delinquencies across government, corporate, credit card, and commercial real estate debt.
* **A Widespread "I Told You So" Moment:** Oliver expects these market shifts to unfold rapidly, predicting that by the end of the year, a global rethink of central banking, fiat currency, and political systems will be well underway.
