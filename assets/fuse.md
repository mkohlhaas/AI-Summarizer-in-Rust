========= The Fuse Is Lit Shock US Decision for Full Fiat Destruction.srt =========

### Overview

In this episode of the *Daniela Cambone Show*, host Daniela Cambone interviews veteran market strategist Gareth Soloway to dissect unprecedented global currency maneuvers and their ripple effects on hard assets. Soloway analyzes the coordinated U.S.-Japan yen intervention, the mounting risks of "defiatization," and provides updated technical forecasts for gold, silver, and Bitcoin.

---

### Core Takeaways

* **Fiat Domino Effect:** The coordinated U.S.-Japan intervention—specifically the U.S. selling Euros to prop up the Yen—proves global powers fear a single currency collapse could trigger a systemic fiat meltdown.
* **Bearish on the Dollar:** Skyrocketing debt and looming tariffs are weakening the U.S. dollar's dominance, driving global players to diversify into the Euro, gold, and other hard assets. 
* **Gold's $13K Target:** Driven by spiraling global debt, Soloway projects gold will embark on a massive bull run peaking at $13,000+ between 2029 and 2031, with $3,500 serving as a major long-term buying floor.
* **Silver Needs $64:** While silver hit Soloway's target of $54, it remains vulnerable to an economic slowdown (with a downside risk to $50) unless it can break and hold above the $64 resistance level.
* **Bitcoin's "Fake-Out" Bounce:** Bitcoin is poised for a short-term rally toward $71,000–$72,000, but Soloway warns it remains in a four-year cycle drawdown that will likely flush prices below $50,000 before a true bottom is found.
* **The Overleveraging Threat:** High concentration and overleveraging of Bitcoin by single entities (such as MicroStrategy) present an existential, systemic risk to the crypto market.
* **Revealing Their Hand:** Unprecedented central bank interventions expose deep underlying panic among global governments, serving as the ultimate long-term bullish signal for gold, silver, and Bitcoin.
