========= Don Durretts Top 12 Gold Silver Stock Picks.srt =========

### Overview

In this interview, gold and silver speculator Don Durrett, founder of goldstockdata.com, outlines his highly bullish thesis on precious metals. Durrett argues that the Federal Reserve is trapped in an impossible dilemma between fighting inflation and sustaining economic growth, which is quietly driving global central banks to accumulate gold. Operating as a pure speculator targeting "five-bagger" (500%+) returns, Durrett maps out his multi-stage market exit strategy and shares his top stock categories, including his preferred mid-tier producers, developers, and silver miners.

---

### Key Takeaways

* **The Fed’s Double Bind:** The Federal Reserve is trapped by over $1 trillion in annual interest payments on national debt. It must choose between fighting inflation or stimulating growth—and either choice will trigger a financial crisis.
* **Western Under-allocation:** While Eastern nations and central banks aggressively stack gold, Western wealth remains dangerously exposed; a survey of 300 affluent family offices revealed that 70% hold zero gold.
* **The "Three Legs" Exit Strategy:** Durrett plans to stop buying during "Leg 2" (expected in Q4 as miners reach fair valuation) and will sell his entire portfolio during "Leg 3" when gold reaches his target of $7,500–$8,000. 
* **Leveraged "Mormon" Silver Stocks:** Durrett favors a specific group of heavily beaten-down silver miners nicknamed "The Mormons"—including Coeur Mining, Hecla, and Pan American Silver—to capture extreme leverage when silver surges.
* **Avoid Royalties and Pure Exploration:** Because he chases maximum upside ("alpha"), Durrett avoids conservative royalty companies and warns against chasing high-risk exploration drill holes during a bull market.
* **Focus on Growth Pipelines:** The ideal risk-reward setup belongs to undervalued producers with clear, near-term production growth and developers slated for mine construction before 2028.
* **Top Speculative Picks:** Durrett highlights several high-potential names from his personal checklist, including Honey Badger, Denarius, Silver Co, Talisker Resources, Jaguar Mining, and Guanajuato.
