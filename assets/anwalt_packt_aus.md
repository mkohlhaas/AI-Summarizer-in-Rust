========= Was ich in deutschen Gerichtssälen erlebt habe Anwalt packt aus.srt =========

### Overview
This interview critiques the declining objectivity of the German judicial system, highlighting how political bias, systemic inefficiencies, and disproportionate state actions degrade public trust, waste vital resources, and inflict unnecessary human suffering.

### Core Points

* **Evidence as a Shield Against Harm:** Video recordings are increasingly essential to expose false testimonies and protect citizens from the severe personal and societal damage of wrongful convictions.
* **Maximizing Public Accountability:** A landmark ruling confirming the legality of filming police in public spaces enhances transparency, reducing the potential for arbitrary abuse of state power.
* **The High Cost of Silencing Speech:** The rise in prosecutions for opinion-based offenses chills open discourse and diverts valuable judicial resources away from preventing actual, tangible harm.
* **Decline in Judicial Competence:** A growing lack of rigorous preparation and objectivity, particularly among younger judges, weakens the judiciary’s function as a vital check on government overreach.
* **Systemic Threat to Democratic Utility:** Allowing administrative committees to exclude political candidates from elections without immediate judicial review undermines the democratic stability necessary for societal welfare.
* **Disproportionate Pre-Trial Detention:** Holding non-violent individuals in custody for over three years based on highly unrealistic "fantasy" coup plots produces immense human suffering without delivering any real public safety benefit.
* **Inefficient Resource Allocation:** The state's aggressive focus on prosecuting conspiracy theorists contrasted with more lenient treatment of actual violent offenders represents a severe misalignment of corrective justice.
* **Preserving the Ultimate Social Safeguard:** The erosion of *in dubio pro reo* (innocent until proven guilty) threatens to strip society of its final protection against state-driven utility-destroying tyranny.
