### Overview

In this interview with *Liberty and Finance*, international economist David Wu discusses the impending collapse of the U.S. artificial intelligence (AI) bubble, mounting U.S. fiscal instability, and the long-term outlook for gold. Wu argues that aggressive Chinese technological innovation, soaring long-term bond yields, and structural U.S. debt will ultimately pop the AI bubble, paving the way for a massive macroeconomic shift and a major bull run for gold.

### Core Points

* **The AI Bubble is Bursting:** The U.S. AI boom is an unsustainable monopoly chain dependent on OpenAI and Anthropic. The bubble is deflating as Chinese competitors deliver equivalent model performance at 40% of the cost—without requiring America's most advanced, expensive chips.
* **China is Winning the Tech Race:** China is outperforming the U.S. in AI, electric vehicles, humanoid robots, and space technology because they foster fierce domestic market competition rather than protecting domestic monopolies. 
* **The AI Crash is Bullish for Gold:** The AI capital expenditure boom artificially pushed real bond yields up, creating a major headwind for gold. Once the tech bubble bursts and the Nasdaq drops 17% to 20%, yields will fall, triggering a massive rotation into gold.
* **Strong Sovereign Support for Gold:** Patient central banks and sovereign entities are aggressively buying gold whenever it dips to the $4,000 level, establishing a firm floor for the precious metal despite temporary global headwinds.
* **Oil Prices are Deflationary:** Contrary to consensus fears, higher oil prices act as a deflationary force on core inflation because expensive gasoline saps consumer discretionary income, lowering demand for other core goods.
* **U.S. Fiscal Health is "Scary":** The U.S. budget deficit is ballooning due to soaring defense spending demands and a Supreme Court ruling declaring certain tariffs illegal (forcing taxpayer refunds). 
* **China is Dumping Treasuries:** Fearing potential asset confiscation by the U.S. government, China is selling off U.S. Treasuries every single month, putting upward pressure on long-term bond yields. 
* **Praise for Fed's Policy Shift:** Wu commends the Federal Reserve for moving away from "forward guidance," arguing that feeding the market constant promises only encourages excessive risk-taking and asset bubbles.
