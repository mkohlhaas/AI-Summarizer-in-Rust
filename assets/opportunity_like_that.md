========= An Opportunity Like This Wont Happen Again for Silver Gold.srt =========

### Overview
Financial technical analyst Jordan Roy argues that the severe correction in the precious metals market has ended, declaring that the bottom is officially in for gold, silver, and mining stocks. Drawing on historical analogs, macroeconomic fundamentals, and deeply depressed sentiment indicators, Roy outlines a highly bullish outlook for the ongoing secular bull market and advises investors to steadily accumulate quality assets during periods of weakness.

### Key Points

* **The Bottom is Confirmed:** Historical comparisons with post-breakout corrections from 1973, 2006, and 2008 strongly indicate that the recent drawdown—which briefly saw gold dip just under $4,000—has bottomed out.
* **Massive Long-Term Price Targets:** While near-term consolidation is likely, historical models project gold breaking out past $6,000 by mid-2025, with potential targets of $7,000 to $8,000 by late 2027 or early 2028.
* **Bullish Macro Fundamentals:** The steepening yield curve (reflecting rising inflation expectations and economic pessimism) and a peaking 2-year yield provide robust fundamental support for a sustained precious metals rally.
* **Key Near-Term Resistance:** Gold faces immediate resistance at $4,340 and its 200-day moving average at $4,481, while silver faces resistance at $70 with a medium-term rebound target of $75. 
* **Depressed Sentiment & Allocation:** Current investor allocations to gold, silver, and mining ETFs relative to total market assets remain near historic, decade-plus lows, signaling massive untapped upside in this secular bull market.
* **Mining Stocks Flash Buy Signals:** A massive ~22% one-week surge in the Huey Gold Bugs Index, paired with extremely oversold breadth indicators, historically aligns with major, multi-year sector bottoms.
* **Accumulation Strategy:** Roy warns against trying to perfectly time the market or waiting for extreme downside targets; instead, he advocates a disciplined "buy, hold, and trim" strategy on price dips.
