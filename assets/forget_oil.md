========= Forget Oil The Next Global Resource Crisis Has Begun Ian Harris.srt =========

### Overview

In this interview, Ian Harris, CEO of Copper Giant, outlines the powerful structural bull case for copper. He discusses how the metal is transitioning from a basic economic indicator ("Dr. Copper") into a critical strategic asset fueled by global electrification and the AI data center buildout. Harris highlights the severe supply-demand mismatch in the copper market and explains how Copper Giant is positioned to capitalize on these trends, particularly with a favorable political shift in Colombia and an upcoming Preliminary Economic Assessment (PEA).

### Key Takeaways

* **Unstoppable Structural Demand:** Copper is breaking out of its traditional cyclical role, driven by the massive infrastructure demands of global electrification, the green energy transition, and the accelerating AI race.
* **Severe Supply Deficit:** Unlike other critical minerals where processing is the main bottleneck, copper suffers from a fundamental shortage of actual deposits; new mines can take up to 20 years to develop.
* **The "New Oil":** Geopolitical risk is rising as the world realizes how concentrated the copper supply chain is—China controls roughly 60% of smelting, while Chile and Peru dominate production.
* **Geological Concentration:** The Andes mountain range remains the world's premier copper belt, hosting 12 of the 20 largest copper mines due to unique subduction zone geology.
* **Colombia’s Political Catalyst:** The election of a new, pro-mining president in Colombia has opened a crucial, time-sensitive window to de-risk and fast-track resource development.
* **Copper Giant’s Year-End Milestones:** Copper Giant (TSXV: CGNT) is running an active drilling program and preparing its first Preliminary Economic Assessment (PEA) by the end of the year to establish the net present value (NPV) of its highly coveted, near-surface project.
