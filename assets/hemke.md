========= Craig Hemke - Fundamental and Technical Drivers Behind the Preci.srt =========

### Overview

In this editorial from *The K Report*, Craig Hemke, founder of the *TF Metals Report*, discusses the strong rebound in precious metals and mining equities. Hemke attributes the market shift to a combination of geopolitical interventions, shifting bond yields, and key technical breakouts, suggesting that the worst of the recent correction is now behind us.

### Core Points

* **The Bottom is In:** Gold and silver established solid floors in late June ($4,000/oz and $55–$56/oz respectively), signaling the end of the market's "peak hawkishness" phase.
* **Yen Support Crushes the Dollar:** Joint efforts by the US Treasury and the Bank of Japan to support the Yen reversed the Dollar Index (DXY) rally, driving it back below 100 and boosting metals. 
* **Falling Bond Yields:** Shifting yields on 2-year and 10-year US Treasuries have reduced expectations for higher positive real interest rates, creating a tailwind for gold.
* **Algos Flip to Buy Mode:** Gold breaking above its 50-day moving average (~$4,250) and mining ETFs (GDX/GDXJ) crossing their 50-week MAs have triggered short-covering and shifted high-frequency trading algorithms from "sell the rip" to "buy the dip."
* **Exploding US Debt:** The Treasury's strategy of funding via short-term T-bills to suppress rates is accelerating the US national debt toward the $40 trillion threshold, highlighting gold's macroeconomic appeal.
* **Gradual Accumulation Over V-Shape:** While the bearish trend has broken, the recovery is expected to be a gradual, sideways-to-higher transition rather than a sharp V-shaped rebound.
* **Key Catalysts to Watch:** The upcoming July non-farm payrolls (jobs) report and the Federal Reserve’s Jackson Hole Symposium in late August will dictate short-term rate expectations and price volatility.
